Open Position: Senior Postgres DBA

About the work

We are looking for a talented and experienced PostgreSQL Database Administrator (DBA) to manage, optimize, and maintain our PostgreSQL databases in a high-performance and mission-critical environment.

You will work closely with teams across the organization to ensure database availability, performance, security, and scalability.

The ideal candidate will have strong knowledge in database tuning, backups, high availability, monitoring systems, and automation (especially with tools like Ansible). Familiarity with containerization technologies such as Kubernetes will be considered a valuable asset to this role.

You will manage PostgreSQL databases across multiple product lifecycle environments—from development to mission-critical production systems—while configuring and maintaining database servers and processes. This position is to work completely remote.

About the Team

We like open source, we develop open-source software, and we are very active and well known at the Postgres community. We build very innovative projects in the Postgres ecosystem and we are the founders of Fundación PostgreSQL and Postgres Ibiza.

The OnGres team is composed of three main profiles:

  • DBA experts from Postgres, many of whom are Speakers at Postgres conferences around the world.
  • DevOps with experience. Troubleshooters, with automation in your heart and Linux as your world.

The Day to day of a DBA at OnGres

A DBA in OnGres is like the guardian of an organization's most valuable digital asset: its data. Their day-to-day is a dynamic blend of proactive maintenance, reactive problem-solving, and strategic planning, ensuring that information flows smoothly and securely.

The morning often begins with a systematic health check. This involves reviewing automated alerts, checking logs for unusual activity, and monitoring database performance metrics like CPU usage, memory allocation, and disk I/O. Are there any slow-running queries? Is storage reaching critical levels? These early observations guide the day's priorities.

A significant portion of time is dedicated to performance tuning. This is a continuous quest for efficiency. They might analyze complex SQL queries to identify bottlenecks, create or modify indexes to speed up data retrieval, or adjust database configurations to optimize resource utilization. It's a bit like being a meticulous mechanic, always looking for ways to make the engine run smoother and faster.

Security is paramount. DBAs are constantly vigilant against unauthorized access and data breaches. This involves managing user permissions, implementing robust authentication mechanisms, applying security patches, and regularly auditing access logs. They are the digital bouncers, ensuring only the right people get into the data club.

Then there's the essential work of backup and recovery. A DBA ensures that comprehensive backup strategies are in place and regularly tested. If a disaster strikes – be it hardware failure, human error, or a cyberattack – they are the first responders, tasked with restoring the database to its last known good state, minimizing downtime and data loss.

Throughout the day, the DBAs are also involved in problem-solving. This can range from troubleshooting application connectivity issues to diagnosing database crashes or resolving data integrity problems. They often collaborate closely with developers, system administrators, and business users to understand and address issues promptly.
