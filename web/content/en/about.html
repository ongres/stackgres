About

The StackGres Project

StackGres is an Open Source project, developed in the open, with a wide and diverse Community of users and developers. The StackGres project welcomes anyone, users, developers to contribute and join the Community.

StackGres as a project originated and is as of today maintained by StackGres, a Postgres specialized company. StackGres is a registered trademark of StackGres, Inc.
